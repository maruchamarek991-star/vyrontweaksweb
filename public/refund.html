Refund Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Vyron Tweaks is a digital product. Because it is delivered right after your payment is confirmed, we handle refunds as described below.

1. When we refund

We will refund your order if you request it within 7 days of delivery and one of these applies:

  • We could not deliver the product after your payment was confirmed.
  • Your license does not work and we cannot fix it after reasonable support attempts.
  • The Product is technically incompatible with your system and we cannot resolve it.
  • You were charged twice or paid more than the order total.

2. When we cannot refund

  • You changed your mind after the product was delivered and activated.
  • Performance gains were smaller than you expected. Results depend on your hardware and setup.
  • Your license was revoked because of a breach of the Terms of Service, such as sharing or reselling a key.
  • A game, anti-cheat or other third-party service took action on your account.
  • Cryptocurrency was sent to a wrong address, on a wrong network, or in a wrong amount.

3. Right of withdrawal (EU / UK customers)

If you live in the EU or UK you normally have 14 days to withdraw from an online purchase. For digital content that right ends once delivery begins, if you asked us to start it and acknowledged that you lose the right of withdrawal. By asking us to deliver the Product in your Discord ticket you give that consent and acknowledgment. This does not affect your other legal rights, for example if the Product is faulty.

4. How to request a refund

Open a ticket on our Discord server and include your order number and a short description of the problem. We will review it and reply in the ticket.

5. How refunds are paid

We refund through the original payment method where possible. Crypto refunds are sent to a wallet address you provide, and network fees may apply. Gift card payments are refunded in a way we agree with you in the ticket.

6. Contact

Questions about this document? Open a ticket on our Discord server.
