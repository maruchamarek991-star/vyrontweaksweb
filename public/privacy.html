Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy explains what information the Vyron website and Vyron Tweaks software process, why, and what choices you have. In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies.

1. Information we process

  • Website visits. Our hosting provider (Vercel) processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and requested pages in server logs to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Orders. When you choose a payment method the website generates an order number and the amount to pay. The website itself does not store orders in a customer database. For crypto payments our server reads the public blockchain to see whether a transfer of the exact amount has arrived; this does not require any personal data.
  • Discord tickets. Payments are confirmed and products are delivered in Discord tickets. There we can see your Discord username and ID and whatever you send us, such as your order number, a transaction ID or payment details you choose to share. Discord's own privacy policy applies to that service.
  • Licensing. The Product verifies your license through KeyAuth, a third-party licensing service. This may involve your license key, a hardware identifier of your PC and your IP address.
  • Payments. Cryptocurrency transactions are public on the blockchain. If you pay with PayPal, PayPal shares limited payment details with us under its own policy.

2. Data stored in your browser

The website saves up to three small items in your browser's local storage: your light/dark theme choice, your language choice and, if you receive one, your discount code with its expiry. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can clear this data at any time in your browser settings.

3. Third-party services

To display the site your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address. Our server fetches cryptocurrency exchange rates from public price APIs to show the amount to pay, and the public member count of our Discord invite to show our community size; your personal data is not sent to them. To check crypto payments our server queries public blockchain nodes and explorers (for example a Solana RPC node and Litecoin explorers); only our own wallet address is looked up. To keep the daily discount fair, our server briefly uses your IP address and browser type to calculate it; this is not stored.

4. Why we use information

  • To process orders, confirm payments, deliver the Product and give support.
  • To verify and protect licenses and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To keep the website running and secure.
  • To meet legal obligations.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your information. It is shared only with the service providers needed to run the shop (hosting, Discord, KeyAuth, payment providers) or where the law requires it. These providers may process data outside your country.

6. How long we keep it

We keep ticket records for as long as needed to give support, verify your purchase and prevent fraud. Blockchain transactions cannot be deleted. You can ask us to delete other information we hold about you.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, EEA or UK) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To use these rights, open a ticket on Discord and we will respond.

8. Children

The Product is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

10. Contact

Questions about this document? Open a ticket on our Discord server.
